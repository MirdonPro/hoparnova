THE PROJECT

HoparNova turns place into sound.

HoparNova is an independent Yerevan-rooted music project released through Mirdon Production. Its catalog moves between jazz, electronic fusion, New Age and hip-hop while returning to a shared vocabulary: Armenian streets and stone, late-night cafés, landscape, memory, smoke, movement and the atmosphere of the city after dark.

HoparNova creates custom-composed music environments for restaurants, cafés, hotels, lounges, retail, offices, events and brands.
