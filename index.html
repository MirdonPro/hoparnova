01 / IDENTITY

A city becomes
a sound system.

The strongest through-line in HoparNova’s catalog is place. Yerevan is not background scenery—it is the instrument.

The debut Yerevan Fusion Flow maps lounges, avenues, landmarks and sunset moods through jazz and electronic fusion. Stoneborn turns toward historic Armenian geographies and remembrance. Hookah Time, with Malo Mnimo, pivots into a compact hip-hop/rap vocabulary. In 2026, Yerevan Jazz Cafe (After Hours) returns to the city at night: coffee, rain, candlelight, Ararat and quiet streets.
